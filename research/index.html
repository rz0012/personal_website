2025 · Stangl Lab

Intracranial Theta & Navigation

Full pipeline for NeuroPace RNS iEEG, from artifact rejection to theta bouts measured against the aperiodic background and related to behavior.

  • Per-channel artifact and epileptiform-transient rejection, detrending, line-noise removal and bandpass filtering within the RNS bandwidth.
  • Theta-band (~4–12 Hz) instantaneous amplitude and phase from time-frequency decomposition, with oscillatory bouts quantified against a fitted 1/f background.
  • Synchronized neural, behavioral and positional streams. Circular–linear statistics relate theta to distance-to-target and spatial location.
2025 · Sen Lab

Attention in Auditory Scene Analysis

Cortical E/I network in which top-down attention gates inhibitory interneurons to suppress competing talkers.

  • Spatial-cue front end built from binaural ITD/ILD signals feeds excitatory–inhibitory cortical units, so attentional gain acts on spatially indexed channels.
  • Evaluated with spike-timing-based measures, not firing rate alone: how decodable the target stays as competitors are added across the spatial grid.
  • A route toward biologically grounded sound segregation for hearing-assistive devices in multi-talker environments.

ECCV-W 2024 · co-first author

Online Stochastic Optimization for Temporal Data

Self-supervised incremental learning on non-stationary, temporally correlated data streams of the kind open-world agents see.

Paper ↗

Agents in the open world see non-stationary, temporally correlated streams. Standard SGD assumes i.i.d. samples from a stationary distribution. Co-designed with Shivang Patel, this framework learns quickly and stays stable on data with temporal dependencies.

WACV 2025 · first author

SiamABC: Efficient Robust Tracking

A real-time Siamese tracker that adapts at test time to adverse visibility on CPU-only edge devices.

Project ↗Paper ↗
  • Efficient trackers overfit to in-distribution data. SiamABC improves accuracy markedly on out-of-distribution sequences.
  • A forward-only, backward-free update rule on batch-norm statistics adapts to the incoming stream at negligible latency.
  • CPU-efficient template–search attention and new self-supervised losses offset the limited capacity of efficient trackers.
ISBI 2024 · co-first author

Morphology-Independent Cell Segmentation

Few-shot domain adaptation for elongated, non-convex cells such as bacteria, built on Omnipose with specialized contrastive losses.

Paper ↗

Retraining a segmentation model for every new microscopy collection costs annotation and compute. With Voke Brume, I designed contrastive losses that adapt to the target domain with pseudo-labels while staying anchored to the source. The result is clearly better segmentation of non-convex cell instances from only a few labeled samples.

WACV 2023 · Oral

CellTranspose

Few-shot domain-adaptive instance segmentation for convex cells, extended to 3D volumes and integrated into syGlass.

Paper ↗
  • Co-designed with Matthew Keaton. Instead of chasing one fully general model, the method adapts to novel data from a handful of annotations.
  • Extended to 3D volumes and integrated into syGlass for VR microscopy quantification, shown at SfN 2022 and 2023.
  • A follow-up contrastive representation loss earned a Trainee Highlight Award at the 9th Annual BRAIN Initiative Meeting.

2025 · Sanofi

Predictive Maintenance Forecasting

VAE-LSTM anomaly detection on vaccine filling-line telemetry, deployed for real-time streaming inference.

  • Continuous multivariate sensor telemetry in Seeq, cleaned for irregular sampling, sensor drift and missing segments.
  • A VAE-based LSTM learns a latent model of nominal machine behavior and flags deviations through reconstruction and prediction residuals, with no labeled fault data needed.
  • ONNX models deployed to the Seeq/Sanofi cloud for live predictive-maintenance decisions.
Project

Predictive Coding from Shunting Networks

A receptive-field shunting network with Hebbian-like learning, in which predictive coding emerges from the formulation itself.

Report ↗

A receptive-field-based shunting network with neurophysiologically valid dynamics and Hebbian-like learning for object recognition. Predictive coding emerged from the formulation, and a formal derivation produced a neurophysiologically plausible training procedure for deep networks.

Project

Continual Bayesian Learning with Spikes

Biologically plausible Bayesian learning for continual adaptation, using a LIF-like variational threshold extended to spiking networks.

Report ↗

A biologically plausible Bayesian learning framework for continual adaptability and reasoning under uncertainty. An adaptive thresholding mechanism acts as a variational activation that mimics leaky integrate-and-fire dynamics, and it was extended to spiking neural networks for neuromorphic hardware.

Project

Test-Time Adaptive Cellpose

Entropy- and contrastive-inspired losses that improve cell segmentation with no new labels and no source data.

A test-time adaptation method for cellular instance segmentation on top of Cellpose. New losses inspired by Shannon entropy and self-supervised contrastive learning improve performance without additional labels or access to the source data.

CVPR-W 2021

Fine-Grained Plant Classification

Uses object detection as attention across 1,000 species and five plant organs, and released a long-tailed benchmark.

Paper ↗

A bottom-up approach that detects plant organs (leaf, flower, fruit, bark, high-density leaves) and fuses the predictions of a variable number of organ-based species classifiers. This counters data variability and long-tail effects. Presented at WVU 2020, URDC 2021 and NCUR 2021.
